# Eolas Observatory

**Journal · Atlas · Alchemy**

A personal observatory for noticing experience, mapping connections, and preserving selected transformations.

## The three instruments

- **Journal** is where experience enters. It contains the daily Prompt Cards and calendar.
- **Atlas** is where relationships can become visible: recurring patterns, contradictions, mapped objects, monthly synthesis, and Prediction ↔ Reality. A connection may remain tentative.
- **Alchemy** is where selected material is transformed into an artifact worth preserving.

These are connected instruments, not a required pipeline. Nothing has to graduate from Journal to Atlas to Alchemy.

## Public / private boundary

The public repository contains the static website, curated Prompt Cards, deliberately public Atlas objects, and Alchemy artifacts that have been **explicitly chosen for public display**.

Alchemy artifacts are **private by default**. Preserving an artifact and publishing it are separate choices. Public artifact metadata may acknowledge that something emerged from journaling without publishing the source journal entry, therapy material, private analysis, or other sensitive context.

Raw journal entries, therapy-session notes, private monthly analysis, habit data, and the private framework working repository are not stored here.

## Operating principles

Prompts are invitations rather than assignments. Quantities provide scaffolding, not quotas. Repetition can be evidence of a pattern without becoming an explanation of cause. The system observes before it optimizes.

## September 2026 interface experiment

September uses a restrained seasonal field palette layered over the persistent Eolas identity: parchment/ink neutrals, lavender and jade continuity, with subtle sage, plum, and dry-gold accents. Appearance supports system, light, and dark modes. Future monthly atmospheres should respond to actual use rather than being designed in advance.

Completion checkboxes for 750 Words and Pixa are browser-local convenience markers. They are not canonical Observatory data and are not stored in this repository.
